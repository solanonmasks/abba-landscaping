What we build

Five trades, one crew. Most of our jobs combine two or three of them.

We also do irrigation, landscape lighting and water features.

Hardscapes and Retaining Walls

Block, concrete, timber and boulder walls that turn a slope into usable yard. Drain rock and pipe behind every one, so water never pushes them over.

  • Engineered walls over 4 ft
  • Terracing and stone steps
  • Drainage built in
  • Cap lights and wiring
Get a Free Estimate Most walls: $8,000 to $45,000

Paver Driveways and Patios

Pavers fail when the base is thin. We dig 8 to 12 inches, compact gravel in lifts, then lay. Two winters later, nothing has moved.

  • Old concrete removed and hauled
  • Permeable paver options
  • Natural stone and porcelain
  • Polymeric sand and sealing
Get a Free Estimate Most jobs: $12,000 to $60,000

Synthetic Turf

Green through a West Coast winter and dry an hour after rain. Good for dogs, kids, and shady yards where grass gives up.

  • Sod removal and grading
  • Compacted gravel base
  • Pet turf with odour-control infill
  • Putting greens
Get a Free Estimate Most jobs: $6,000 to $25,000

Outdoor Carpentry

Fences, decks, pergolas and gazebos in cedar, pressure-treated or composite. Built by the same crew as your hardscape, so the levels line up.

  • Cedar and composite decks
  • Privacy fences and gates
  • Pergolas and gazebos
  • Benches and planters
Get a Free Estimate Most jobs: $9,000 to $50,000

Masonry and Stone Veneer
